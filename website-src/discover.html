DAYLIGHT / RASPBERRY PI

Linux, your way.

A lightweight Wayland desktop with labwc, Conky and Daylight’s Rust tools for 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS. Free software, yours to customise, installed with APT.

Rediscover Linux

A straightforward menu, a terminal close at hand and readable configuration files. Explore a desktop you can understand.

Make your Pi your own

Choose a landscape, a Conky profile and your tools. Keep Raspberry Pi OS as the foundation instead of replacing everything.

Build with the project

Read the Rust, test on your hardware and contribute an improvement. Curious beginners and experienced users are welcome.

What you will find

A labwc desktop on Wayland or Fluxbox on X11, a Conky monitor, Daylight landscapes and an ASCII-branded terminal. Rust utilities cover passwords, temperatures, dates, local networking and alarms; six illustrated games are included with the desktop. The --all option adds four more games. Graphical interfaces use Tk, GTK or YAD depending on the application.

A familiar foundation, not a black box

Raspberry Pi OS remains identifiable. Daylight is managed as Debian packages and keeps its settings separate. Its code and tests are public under GPLv3, with third-party licenses preserved.

Lightweight: a goal to verify

No Electron-based toolkit, no permanent settings server and no network request whenever you open a terminal. Results depend on hardware and running services: we do not promise a speedup percentage without comparable measurements.
