Become a Daylight GNU/Linux mirror

Help distribute signed free software using your own HTTPS server. Registration is free.

Set up your mirror

  1. Prepare a public HTTPS web server with enough space for the APT repository.
  2. Follow the installation guide and verify the official signing key fingerprint.
  3. Sync the signed repository and check that your /dists/daylight/InRelease file is reachable.
  4. Request registration on GitHub, or email info@daylightlinux.ch.
  5. After approval, the health monitor checks every 15 minutes. Your mirror is listed after 24 consecutive hours of success, and hidden automatically if it fails a check.

Active community mirrors

The public registry is available at community-mirrors.json. Only validated active mirrors appear there. No community mirrors have been registered yet.

Mirror registration is separate from Daylight telemetry. No client is automatically redirected to an unverified community mirror.
