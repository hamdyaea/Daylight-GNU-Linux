Become a Daylight GNU/Linux mirror

Help distribute free software. Host a copy of our signed APT repository on your own HTTPS server.

How to join

  1. Prepare a publicly reachable HTTPS web server with enough storage for the full repository.
  2. Follow the mirror installation guide. Pin the official archive signing key before syncing.
  3. After the first successful synchronization, request registration by email with your mirror URL, country and optional public name.
  4. We verify your mirror periodically. It is listed only after 24 consecutive hours of successful checks and hidden whenever it is unhealthy.

Registration is voluntary. Your email address is not published. Mirrors must serve the original signed repository without modifying packages.
