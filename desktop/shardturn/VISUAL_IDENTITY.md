# Shardturn — The Color Card Duel

**Shardturn** combines **shard**, a small angular fragment, with **turn**, the rhythm of a card game. The name connects the faceted emblem to changing colors and alternating moves.

## Emblem and wordmark

The emblem brings together four angular facets around an open center. Each facet uses the normalized points `(0, -1)`, `(0.86, -0.18)`, `(0.12, -0.09)` and `(-0.34, -0.5)`, rotated by 0°, 90°, 180° or 270°. This is the same construction drawn by `Painter.emblem` in the interface.

The wordmark **SHARDTURN** uses upright capitals with the tagline **THE COLOR CARD DUEL**. The reference typeface is **DejaVu Sans**. The cards use a warm paper background, a color bar and a family marker. Their backs combine a night background, a diamond lattice, the emblem and the **ST** monogram. The composition uses no diagonal oval or commercial game logo.

## Four families

Every family has a shape as well as a color, making the cards easier to distinguish.

| Family | Exact color | Marker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coral | `#ce6655` | Circle |
| Amber | `#c79a36` | Diamond |
| Jade | `#358d7d` | Triangle |
| Iris | `#7c6fbb` | Square |

## Announcement and assets

The announcement for a one-card hand is **LAST CARD!**, with keyboard shortcut `L`. Action cards keep readable labels: **PAUSE**, **REVERSE**, **WILD**, **+2** and **+4**.

- `assets/shardturn-icon.svg`: square icon on a night background.
- `assets/shardturn-logo.svg`: emblem, wordmark and tagline on warm ivory.

Both assets are scalable vector files with no external images. The name received a preliminary web search; this does not replace a search of trademark registers.
